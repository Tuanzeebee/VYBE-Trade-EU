## 3. Data We Collect

Identity & account data: name, title, email, phone, login credentials.

Business profile & evidence data: licenses, tax/VAT/EORI numbers, certificates, uploaded documents (which may contain signatories' personal data).

Communications data: RFQ content, messages, connection requests.

Profile view data: when a signed-in user opens a supplier's public profile, we record the viewing business and the time of the view. The supplier sees the viewing business's name only if it is a verified buyer that has not turned on anonymous viewing; all other views are shown only as counts. Buyers can turn anonymous viewing on or off at any time in their Company profile. [Retention period for view data: to be decided.]

Usage & technical data: access logs, IP address, device, cookies for operation and security.

## 4. Purposes and Legal Bases

Operating accounts & connections (basis: performance of contract/terms).

Verifying enterprises & displaying trust levels (basis: legitimate interest and/or consent).

Security, fraud prevention, legal compliance (basis: legal obligation & legitimate interest).

Service improvement & support contact (basis: legitimate interest/consent).

Showing suppliers which buyer businesses viewed their profile (basis: legitimate interest; buyers may object by using anonymous viewing).

Where processing is based on consent, the data subject may withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing).

## 5. Verification and Third-Party Sharing

For verification, we may cross-check data against public sources (e.g. Vietnam's business/tax registries; the EU VAT/EORI checking systems).

We may use processors such as cloud hosting and OCR/AI services to support document processing. They process only on our instructions and under confidentiality; we do not sell personal data.

We may disclose data upon lawful request by competent authorities.

## 6. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Given the VN–EU nature of the service, data may be stored/processed in Vietnam and/or other regions. We apply appropriate safeguards and comply with cross-border transfer requirements under Law 91/2025 and the GDPR (e.g. standard contractual clauses where needed).

## 7. Retention

We retain data for as long as necessary for the collection purposes or as required by law. Verification audit trails may be retained immutably for transparency and legal obligations; when purposes end, data is deleted or anonymized.

## 8. Data Subject Rights

Depending on applicable law, data subjects have the rights to: be informed; access; rectify; erase; restrict/object to processing; withdraw consent; and data portability (under GDPR). To exercise these, contact the DPO in Section 2.

Data subjects may lodge a complaint with the competent authority: in Vietnam, the state authority for personal data protection; in the EU, the data protection Supervisory Authority of their residence.

## 9. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures: transport encryption (HTTPS/TLS), role-based access control (RBAC), separation of evidence storage from the application server, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure; we maintain incident response and notification procedures as required by law.

## 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies/similar technologies for login, security and basic operational analytics. Users can manage cookies via their browser; some cookies are necessary for the Platform to function.
